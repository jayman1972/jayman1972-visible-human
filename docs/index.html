    Take it apart

    • Swipe up on the rail at the right edge to explode the body. Swipe down to put it back together.
    • Drag to rotate. Pinch to zoom in close for full detail. Two fingers to move.
    • Scroll to explode the body, and scroll back to put it together. You can also drag the rail on the right.
    • Drag to rotate. Ctrl + scroll, pinch or the + − buttons to zoom in close for full detail. Right-drag to move. Double-click a part to zoom to it.
    • Point at any part to see its name.
    • Tap any part to learn about it, then Slice it to see inside.
    • Motion starts the heartbeat, breathing, blood flow and more, with sound.
    • Lost? Restore (the house button) puts everything back to the starting view.
    • Ken mode and Barbie mode smooth over the genitals and nipples, like a doll, for a family-friendly view.
    • Install it: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen and works offline.

    About & sources

    An interactive 3D atlas of the human body, made for learning. It is not medical advice.

    Anatomy

    • 3D models: Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA 4.0), built on BodyParts3D © The Database Center for Life Science (CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan). The converted models are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
    • Female body shape: adapted from MakeHuman's average adult man and woman (CC0).
    • Modeled for this app: the female reproductive organs, breast tissue and pudendal region (from standard adult measurements), and the hair.

    Text

    • Atlas notes: adapted from Wikipedia articles bundled with Z-Anatomy (CC BY-SA).
    • Part descriptions and tours: written for this app.

    Software

    • three.js (MIT), postprocessing (Zlib), N8AO (ISC) and meshoptimizer (MIT).
    • Source code: github.com/jayman1972/jayman1972-visible-human
